<objective>
Make the solver able to carry what lowering will give it.

Purpose: `research/package-graphs/DIAGNOSIS.md` §1 and §2: the TS/JS points-to solve is blanked by a global budget latch on every package above about a thousand call sites, and it has no rule that connects a call to its callee's parameters, return and `this`. Nothing lowered in Phase 75 can be measured until both are fixed.
Output: a solver whose budget events are honest and partial, that has the call rule, whose slot identities are stable, and whose result is order-independent; the three large packages solving with their derived edges kept.
</objective>
