    - Source of the constraints. MIR names its places by identifier text, not by the binding a name resolves to, so two `x` in different scopes are one place and a captured variable cannot be told from a shadowing one. The constraints are lowered from the same parse instead, by a walker over oxc's semantic model (`ts/constraints/`), which resolves every reference to its symbol. The walker is total: every expression is either lowered or treated as an unknown operation whose result is open and whose operands escape. MIR keeps its role for summaries and is not changed for this task, so the callee-place change to `MirOperationKind::Call` is not made; the member-callee mutation check becomes "lower member callees without a callee place".

A `CallConstraint` names its callee only where the callee's value is described (a local binding, a parameter, a call result, a member read); an import without a matching export, a global or an unknown operation leaves it unnamed, so an open value is never copied into the callsite and does not demote the exact answers of other callees.
